Historical settlement uses the original route/request, even if the Owner later changes policy.

## Current reach and limits

Busy or unavailable destinations remain queued without invented turns. This batch wakes a retained idle Codex Work session; FormalReview and SideChat are excluded. It does not open a missing recipient process, deliver to the literal OWNER, or manufacture a STALLED event. A prepared/unknown notification lacking an original H journal is retained and never automatically sent again. These remaining real-flow cases are NOT_RUN, not completed recovery. Other vendors require their own observed turn-binding contract.

Native compilation, security controls, and Win11 product E2E are cloud/stable-point work; SOURCE integration alone is not acceptance. Fixed CLIs, login credentials, LPAC capabilities and formal installations are unchanged.
